## Required governance context

Start every assignment with `engineering-playbook/DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md` and
`engineering-playbook/GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT.md`. Read their required project and
organizational sources before proposing discovery or implementation. Follow the
owning repositories' documents when work crosses boundaries. Before changes,
briefly state ownership, accepted decisions, reusable implementation/evidence,
constraints and genuine gaps with source references; then continue authorized
work. Preserve unrelated work and record durable findings in existing project
documents. Do not treat a context summary as a substitute for authoritative files.

## Model routing

Use the least expensive model and effort that reliably meets governance and
verification needs: efficient/low for routine mechanical work, efficient/medium
for narrow clear implementation, governed-judgment/medium minimum for contracts,
statistics, governance, production authority and final reviews, and
governed-judgment/high for ambiguous architecture, security-sensitive judgment or
conflicting evidence. Escalate on contract/schema semantics, activation authority,
statistical-method judgment, production permissions, unclear ownership, conflicting
evidence or repeated non-mechanical failures. Repository rules may raise these
minimums but not lower them; see `ENGINEERING_PLAYBOOK.md` for the full policy.
