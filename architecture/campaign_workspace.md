# Campaign Workspace Pattern

A campaign workspace is a reusable operational-context workspace for a bounded initiative involving multiple entities, stages, or decisions.

It may surface scope, participants, evidence readiness, workflow progress, evaluations, rankings, exceptions, and authorized commands.

It does not own campaign business state, evaluation logic, ranking logic, evidence, or organizational knowledge. It assembles projections from authoritative domains.

Projects may use a different domain term, such as engagement, case, program, study, release, or review cycle.
