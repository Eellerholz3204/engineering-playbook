# Product Vision Pattern

A product vision defines the durable problem and authority model without prematurely locking implementation details.

Required sections:

1. Problem and opportunity.
2. Primary users and operators.
3. Desired business outcomes.
4. Product principles.
5. Human decision authority.
6. Permitted and prohibited AI responsibilities.
7. Core operational surfaces.
8. Non-goals.
9. Success measures.
10. Extensibility and integration posture.
11. Risks and safeguards.

The project-specific realization belongs in `docs/product_vision.md`.
